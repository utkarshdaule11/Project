STOP 01 Northern Origin • Himalayas

Jammu & Kashmir

The Crown of the Subcontinent

The journey begins amidst snow-dusted alpine crests and chilled pine winds. Here, historic Mughal garden terraces slope towards freshwater lakes against the Great Himalayan rampart.

Two Distinct Specialities
  • 01 Kashmiri Saffron & Pashmina Weaving: World-renowned deep crimson Kongh saffron and masterfully spun Pashmina cashmere shawls.
  • 02 Kahwa Tea & Wooden Shikara Heritage: Aromatic green tea brewed with saffron, cinnamon, and almonds, enjoyed upon handcrafted cedarwood houseboats.
Landmark
Dal Lake Shikaras & Floating Heritage
Srinagar, Kashmir Valley
Natural Wonder
Pir Panjal Range & Alpine Pine Meadows
Pahalgam & Gulmarg Highlands
02
STOP 02 Western Corridor • Desert Realm

Rajasthan

Land of Forts, Dunes & Royal Valour

Descending southward into the arid grandeur of the Thar Desert, the highway passes sandstone bastions that stand sentinel over golden sandbanks and rich folklore.

Two Distinct Specialities
  • 01 Rajput Hill Fort Architecture: Impregnable stone fortresses featuring intricate jharokha latticework, stepwells, and royal palaces.
  • 02 Dal Baati Churma & Bandhani Textiles: Iconic desert culinary staples made with pure ghee, paired with vibrant tie-dye artisanal fabric.
Landmark
Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds)
Jaipur, Rajasthan
Natural Wonder
Thar Desert Golden Sand Dunes
Sam & Khuri, Jaisalmer
03
STOP 03 Western Shoreline • White Salt Plains

Gujarat

The Vibrant Coastal Trading Cradle

Tracking along the Gulf of Khambhat and the edge of the Rann, the route encounters white salt wilderness, ancient carved stepwell marvels, and authentic lion reserves.

Two Distinct Specialities
  • 01 Sole Asiatic Lion Sanctuary: The Gir National Park forest ecosystem, preserving the globe's only remaining wild Asiatic lions.
  • 02 Rann Utsav & Patola Silk Craftsmanship: Annual cultural white desert festival combined with intricate double-ikat Patan Patola hand-weaving.
Landmark
Adalaj Ni Vav (Solanki Stepwell Architecture)
Gandhinagar / Ahmedabad, Gujarat
Natural Wonder
White Desert of Kutch (Salt Flats)
Great Rann Salt Marshes, Gujarat
04
STOP 04 Deccan Plateau • Western Ghats

Maharashtra

The Gateway of Fortresses & Modern Energy

The highway winds over the Western Ghats (Sahyadris), cutting through dramatic basalt cliffs, monsoon gorges, and ancient rock-cut cave temples before opening to the Arabian Sea.

Two Distinct Specialities
  • 01 Ancient Rock-Cut Basalt Caves: UNESCO World Heritage cave monasteries at Ajanta, Ellora, and Elephanta carved out of solid volcanic rock.
  • 02 Vada Pav Culture & Alphonso Mangoes: World-famous fiery Mumbai street food and the GI-tagged sweet Ratnagiri Hapus mangoes.
Landmark
Gateway of India & Mumbai Harbour
Colaba, Mumbai, Maharashtra
Natural Wonder
Sahyadri Mountain Escarpment
Western Ghats Ridge, Maharashtra
05
STOP 05 Konkan Coast • Tropical Haven

Goa

Susegad Breezes, Baroque Churches & Golden Palms

Hugging the emerald Konkan coast, the journey eases into peaceful coastal towns lined with Portuguese-era chapels, spice plantations, and sea breezes.

Two Distinct Specialities
  • 01 Portuguese-Baroque Basilica Architecture: Historic Latin quarter settlements and the 16th-century Basilica of Bom Jesus.
  • 02 Goan Fish Curry & Cashew Feni Distilling: Fiery coconut-kokum seafood recipes paired with heritage indigenous cashew liquor distilling.
Landmark
Basilica of Bom Jesus (UNESCO Baroque Church)
Old Goa, Goa
Natural Wonder
Goan Tropical Coastline & Coconut Palms
Palolem & South Goa Beaches
06
STOP 06 Southern Terminus • Cape Comorin

Kanyakumari

Where Three Oceans Meet at Land's End

The grand highway concludes at the absolute southern tip of the Indian mainland. Here, the waters of the Arabian Sea, the Bay of Bengal, and the Indian Ocean converge in panoramic serenity.

Two Distinct Specialities
  • 01 The Triveni Sangam Confluence: The unique marine union of three water bodies where you can watch sunrise and sunset from the same shore.
  • 02 Vivekananda Rock & Thiruvalluvar Memorial: Majestic stone monuments erected on twin offshore sea rocks honouring philosophy and Tamil literature.
Landmark
Vivekananda Rock Memorial & Sea Shrine
Cape Comorin Rock Island, Kanyakumari
Natural Wonder
Triveni Sangam Tri-Ocean Sunset Horizon
Confluence of Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal & Indian Ocean
